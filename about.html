About NightLight Economy

Understanding Earth's economic pulse through satellite imagery

🛰️ What is NightLight Economy?

NightLight Economy is an innovative web platform that leverages NASA's VIIRS (Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite) satellite data to analyze economic activity on Earth through nighttime light observations. Our system provides real-time, unbiased measurements of human activity, urbanization, and infrastructure development.

Unlike traditional economic indicators that are slow to update and often politically influenced, satellite-derived night-light data offers immediate, objective insights into regional development, making it invaluable for policy makers, researchers, and investors worldwide.

🔬 The Science Behind It

NASA VIIRS Data

500m spatial resolution capturing Earth's brightness every night since 2012

AI Analysis

Machine learning algorithms detect anomalies and patterns in brightness data

Real-time Processing

Google Earth Engine processes petabytes of data for instant visualization
